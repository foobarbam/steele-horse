... and a bit more

Three days ago I celebrated by one year anniversary with my current employer.  It
has gone reasonably well all things considered.  I've learned much and added to my
career knowledge base quite a bit.

Pros:

* After more than a decade of remote work its nice to be in an office again.  At
  the end of my remote tenure I had had enough of working from home and my mental 
  health was suffering.  I now get to work with real people instead of slack 
  usernames.
* [OpenShift Openshift Openshift][openshift] - My [Openshift][openshift] 
  [Kubernetes][k8s] knowledge has increased a thousand fold and I can write
  [helm][helm] charts and k8s deployments in my sleep.
* I've made a few frinds in the process.  Rarely leaving the house is not conducive
  to making friends.
* Paychecks are a good thing.
* This jobs is far less stressful than the previous job.
* My employer is a local institution and isn't going anywhere.  It feeds my need for
  civic pride.

Cons:

* I'm not paid what I am worth.  When I decided to take a year off after leaving
  [Armis][armis], I knew that if I got back into the IT field, I would have to
  work my way back up to my previous pay scale.  This was expected but still stings.
  * *Does a man offend your pride? Remember he will be dead soon, as will you.* - Marcus Aurelius
* Things move very slow.  New projects, plans, etc can take forver to be approved
  and implemented.
* I do not feel a sense of empowerment nor do my peers.  There is too much
  micromanagement and not enought trust in us.
* Their IT architecture is antiquated and there is significant resistance to modern
  corporate network concepts and designs.
* Resistance to all things cloud and AI

I hate to go into some of those cons.  Resistance to cloud and AI isn't necessarily
bad.  I myself am not very fond of the AI movement and I think companies were too
eager to move all their computing infratructure into the cloud.
